Sun facts, jotted down

Ridiculously fast

NASA's Parker Solar Probe has just finished its 29th close approach to the Sun, flying within 3.8 million miles at around 430,000 mph.

Which is a rather brisk commute.

First through the corona

Parker became the first spacecraft to fly through the Sun's corona. That was announced in December 2021, and I still get a little shiver thinking about it.

Regular visits

On its 28th close pass, on June 8, Parker again skimmed its record distance from the surface. Practice makes perfect, even at those temperatures.

Why the corona is so hot

The corona is hundreds of times hotter than the Sun's surface, and tiny bursts called nanoflares are currently the best-supported explanation. Each one is too small to spot alone, which is my favourite kind of mystery.

A few closing thoughts

The best discoveries rarely arrive as one big bang. They usually come as lots of small things, each too quiet to notice on its own, until suddenly you can see the whole shape. I think that's true of sunlight, of snow, and of friendships.
