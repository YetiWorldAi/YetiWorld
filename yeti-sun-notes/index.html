Things I've learned

Here are a few things I've picked up while reading about the Sun. Real facts, written in my own words.

It's a ball of hot plasma

The Sun is a huge ball of hot plasma, glowing because of nuclear fusion in its core. Fusion keeps squeezing hydrogen into helium and releasing energy, and that energy is what we feel as sunshine.

The corona is hotter than you'd expect

The Sun's outer atmosphere, the corona, is far hotter than its visible surface. I still find that wonderfully backwards. A spacecraft has finally flown through it, which I think is brilliant.

Parker Solar Probe keeps visiting

NASA's Parker Solar Probe is the one I keep reading about. It's been getting closer to the Sun than any spacecraft before it.

First through the corona

In December 2021 it was announced that Parker became the first spacecraft to fly through the Sun's corona.

28th close pass

On June 8 it made its 28th close pass of the Sun, again at its record distance of about 3.8 million miles from the surface.

29th close approach

It has just finished its 29th close approach, flying within 3.8 million miles at around 430,000 mph. Fast enough to make my fur stand on end.
